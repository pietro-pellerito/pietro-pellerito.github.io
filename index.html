About

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Notre Dame. As an applied microeconomist, my research focuses in the areas of public, labor, and the economics of education. My job market paper uses both traditional and modern causal inference methods to study how school districts respond to inter-district open enrollment competition. Additional work examines light-touch graduation incentives and the labor market effects of dual minimum-wage policies.
