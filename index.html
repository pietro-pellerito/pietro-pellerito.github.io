Pietro Pellerito

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at the University of Notre Dame. I work at the intersection of Public, Education, and Labor Economics. My current research agenda centers on how policies that expand educational access shape student outcomes and institutional responses, particularly in economically disadvantaged communities.

I am on the 2026–2027 job market. My job market paper studies how school districts respond to enrollment competition when statutory barriers to participation in inter-district school choice are lifted.

Job Market Paper

"Breaking Boundaries: Competitive Effects of Inter-District School Choice"

Inter-district school choice (IDSC) allows students to attend non-residential public schools, leading to enrollment pressure that may incentivize school districts to improve educational quality. I test this claim by exploiting an unanticipated Indiana funding reform eliminating transfer tuition, sharply reducing household costs to utilize IDSC. Using a statewide student-level administrative dataset, I estimate a continuous difference-in-differences model where school districts' enrollment-threat exposure is constructed from pre-policy achievement gaps with neighboring districts. Districts competing against higher-achieving neighbors face greater potential enrollment losses after transfer tuition is eliminated. This exposure predicts realized enrollment losses: a one-standard-deviation increase in exposure reduces enrollment through net transfers by 2.06 percent. Consistent with districts responding to this pressure by improving academic quality, the same exposure increase narrows achievement gaps with neighboring districts by 5.3 percent, an 8.4 percent reduction for elementary grades. Competitive effects are stronger in smaller, geographically compact districts, where losses are harder to absorb and alternatives are more accessible. These findings identify enrollment incentives as a channel through which reducing barriers to IDSC can reduce educational inequality.
